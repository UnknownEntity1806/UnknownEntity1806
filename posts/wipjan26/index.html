So I have made some sketches for that. Although I will not start actually working on coding version 2.0 until all the pages are up and running in version 1.2. sketch of a website layout with clouds

It’s hard to see now, but up until now I have included one other fairy and some falling leaves. sketch of a website layout with clouds

So, those are the things I am working on this month. As I am also in the process of moving over to the winter-house, it’s not a lot. But I wanted to give this update in any case.
